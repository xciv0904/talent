# Public Career Naming Audit

## Purpose

The 60 specific Career Profiles remain the calculation source. This audit defines the first user-facing layer: 33 plain-language Public Careers. A group is allowed only when its members share recognizable daily work and overlapping talent requirements; sharing an industry or using a computer is not enough.

## Breadth checks

- Software development, cybersecurity, and data analysis remain separate; “technology” is too broad.
- Product management, project management, and operations management remain separate because their decision scopes differ.
- Direct rehabilitation care, healthcare information, and clinical research remain separate.
- Technical trades are grouped only at the first layer; regulated and tool-specific titles remain visible underneath.
- Every specific career appears exactly once. Tests enforce completeness and prevent duplicate membership.
