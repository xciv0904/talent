# Profile Dimension Boundary Audit

Work Style describes **how a person naturally contributes**. Environment describes **how much of an external condition a job demands and a person can tolerate**. Values describe **why an outcome matters**. These definitions prevent one preference from receiving multiple weights under different names.

| Dimension | Layer | Definition | Nearest concept | Double-counting risk / resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| independent | Work Style | Prefers owning and producing work individually | socialDensity | Medium; independent contribution is not low contact. Measured only in ENV05. |
| collaborative | Work Style | Prefers jointly producing and coordinating work | socialDensity | Medium; collaboration method is distinct from amount of contact. Measured only in ENV05. |
| strategic | Work Style | Prefers setting direction and making trade-offs | ambiguity | Low; strategic contribution can occur in clear or ambiguous settings. |
| hands_on | Work Style | Prefers manipulating, building, or directly executing | mobility | Low; hands-on work can be stationary. |
| detail_focused | Work Style | Prefers depth, accuracy, and craft | structure / repetition | Medium; detail focus is contribution mode, not rule density or repeated workload. |
| facilitative | Work Style | Prefers enabling a group through dialogue and process | emotionalLabor | Medium; facilitation does not imply absorbing sustained emotional demands. |
| socialDensity | Environment | Required frequency and intensity of interpersonal contact | collaborative | Kept separate; assessed by ENV02, not ENV05. |
| pace | Environment | Required speed and frequency of time pressure | strategic | No direct overlap. |
| ambiguity | Environment | Amount of missing definition and changing direction | strategic | Kept separate; tolerance is not skill or preferred contribution. |
| mobility | Environment | Required movement between locations or physical contexts | hands_on | Kept separate; hands-on does not require travel. |
| risk | Environment | Consequence and uncertainty attached to mistakes or decisions | precision | Kept separate; risk is job demand, precision is a Talent. |
| repetition | Environment | Amount of repeated procedures or recurring cycles | detail_focused | Kept separate; repeated work is not the same as caring about detail. |
| emotionalLabor | Environment | Required regulation and absorption of others' emotions | facilitative / emotional_perception | Kept separate from both contribution preference and demonstrated Talent. |
| structure | Environment | Density of rules, procedures, and prescribed methods | conventional / planning | Kept separate; structure is external demand, not Interest or Talent. |
| autonomy | Values | Importance placed on control over choices and direction | independent | Resolved: autonomy appears only in Values; Work Style `independent` describes production mode. |

No Work Style ID is duplicated in Environment. ENV01–ENV04 produce Environment tolerance only; ENV05 produces Work Style only. The match formula therefore gives the two layers separate 15% components without reusing the same signal.
