# Entry Distance Sanity Matrix

Entry Distance is independent of Career Fit. The matrix uses deterministic fictitious readiness profiles and the Career's actual requirements.

| Requirement pattern | Career | User readiness | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional license heavy | Occupational Therapist | Secondary education; no role skills or license | Very High |
| Professional license heavy | Occupational Therapist | Required education, skills, and license | Low |
| Portfolio heavy | UX Designer | Secondary education; no skills or portfolio | Medium |
| Portfolio heavy | UX Designer | Bachelor, required skills, portfolio | Low |
| Experience heavy | Product Manager | Bachelor; no role skills or experience | Medium |
| Experience heavy | Product Manager | Bachelor, required skills, two years experience | Low |
| Technical skill heavy | Cybersecurity Analyst | Secondary education; no skills or Security+ | High |
| Technical skill heavy | Cybersecurity Analyst | Bachelor, required skills, Security+ | Low |
| Education heavy | Policy Researcher | Secondary education; no skills or portfolio | High |
| Education heavy | Policy Researcher | Master, required skills, portfolio | Low |
| Relatively accessible | Guest Experience Manager | Associate, one year, English, partial skills | Medium |
| Relatively accessible | Guest Experience Manager | Associate, two years, English, required skills | Low |

PASS. The matrix produces Low, Medium, High, and Very High. A missing professional license is heavily weighted but does not make unrelated roles Very High. Portfolio-only and experience-only gaps remain Medium when other barriers are moderate. Across all 60 Careers, the novice profile spans at least three levels rather than collapsing to Medium.
